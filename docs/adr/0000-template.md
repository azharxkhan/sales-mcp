# ADR-NNNN: Short title of the decision

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX | Deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who agreed to it
- **Related:** issues, PRs, research docs, other ADRs

## Context

What problem are we solving? Which forces matter (requirements, constraints, research findings, risks)?
Keep it factual and short enough to read in two minutes.

## Decision

What we will do, stated in one or two sentences, then the details that matter.
Write in the active voice: "We will…".

## Consequences

**Positive**
- What gets easier or safer.

**Negative / trade-offs**
- What gets harder, what it costs, what we give up.

**Follow-ups**
- Work this decision creates (issues, milestones, tests).
