# Code of Conduct

This project is also a course, so many people here are learning. Everyone should be able to ask questions, make mistakes and contribute without being made to feel unwelcome.

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with beginners;
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, aimed at the work rather than the person;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community as a whole, not just ourselves.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commit messages. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, please tell the maintainer, [Azhar Khan (@azharxkhan)](https://github.com/azharxkhan), through GitHub:

- For something that can be discussed in the open, comment on the issue or pull request, or open a new issue.
- For anything that needs privacy, open a short issue titled **"Code of Conduct: private contact request"** with no details, and the maintainer will arrange a private channel with you. If the problem involves the maintainer, say so in that issue and it will be handled with extra care.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of the person reporting will be respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take fair corrective action in response to behaviour they consider inappropriate. That may include removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and explaining the reason when it is appropriate.

The maintainer will follow these steps, depending on how serious the behaviour is:

1. **Correction.** For a minor problem, such as inappropriate language: a private written warning explaining what was wrong. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** For a single incident or a series of actions: a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a set time.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour: a temporary ban from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression towards or belittling of groups of people: a permanent ban from any interaction with the project.
